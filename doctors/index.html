Dr. T. VITTAL MOHAN

Director, Consultant Paediatric Surgeon

Dr. T. VITTAL MOHAN

Director, Consultant Paediatric Surgeon

A Lifesaver for Children in Rayalaseema For over 20 years, Dr. T. Vittal Mohan, M.S., M.Ch., has been a beacon of hope for children in the Rayalaseema region of Andhra Pradesh. With a remarkable record of over 40,000 successful surgeries, he has transformed the lives of countless families. Specializing in minimally invasive procedures, he is a pioneer in laparoscopy, addressing appendicitis, hernias, and other abdominal issues with minimal scarring. His expertise in bronchoscopy has enabled life-saving interventions for airway obstructions, including foreign body aspiration. He is also highly skilled in thoracoscopy for chest surgeries, as well as cystoscopy and vesicoscopy for treating complex urinary tract issues. Dr. Mohan’s expertise extends to a wide range of pediatric conditions, including birth defects, trauma, childhood cancers, and complex urological disorders. He is particularly renowned for handling challenging cases such as Primary PSARP without colostomy for anorectal malformations. His commitment to excellence is evident in his numerous publications in international medical journals and his contributions to leading pediatric surgery textbooks. His dedication has been recognized with the prestigious "Best Pediatric Surgical Service Provider of Andhra Pradesh" award from the Andhra Pradesh State NTR Healthcare Trust. "His expertise and compassion are truly exceptional." If you are seeking expert and compassionate care for your child, Dr. T. Vittal Mohan is the ideal choice. Schedule a consultation today to discuss your child's specific needs.

With over a decade of clinical and academic experience, Dr. Rajesh is recognized for his expertise in pediatric and neonatal critical care, simulation-based medical training, and public health advocacy for children. His academic contributions include ongoing and completed research in febrile seizures, microcytic anemia, global developmental delay, and simulation-based neonatal resuscitation. He holds key roles in national and state-level pediatric programs, serving as a National Trainer for both Basic and Advanced Neonatal Resuscitation Programs (IAP-NNF), and as the State Academic Coordinator for IAP-NNF-NRP-FGM in Andhra Pradesh. Dr. Rajesh is an active member of numerous professional organizations including the Indian Academy of Pediatrics, National Neonatology Forum, PEDISTARS, and the Breastfeeding Promotion Network of India (BPNI). His commitment to pediatric health is marked by a blend of clinical excellence, academic leadership, and a strong sense of social responsibility.
