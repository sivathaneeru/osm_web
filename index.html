Dr. T. Vittal Mohan

A Lifesaver for Children in Rayalseema

Dr. T. Vittal Mohan, M.S., M.Ch., is a distinguished Senior Pediatric Surgeon with over two decades of experience and an extraordinary record of more than 40,000 successful surgeries. Renowned for his pioneering work in minimally invasive pediatric procedures, he specializes in laparoscopy, thoracoscopy, bronchoscopy, and advanced urological surgeries. Dr. Mohan is widely respected for his expertise in handling complex cases with precision and compassion, making him a trusted choice for families seeking world-class pediatric surgical care.

For over 20 years, Dr. T. Vittal Mohan has been a beacon of hope for children in the Rayalseema region, Andhra Pradesh. With a remarkable record of over 40,000 successful surgeries, he has transformed the lives of countless families.

Specializing in minimally invasive procedures, Dr. Mohan is a pioneer in:

  • Laparoscopy – Addressing appendicitis, hernias, and abdominal issues with minimal scarring.

  • Bronchoscopy – Performing life-saving interventions for airway obstructions, including foreign body aspiration.

  • Thoracoscopy – Expert in chest surgeries.

  • Cystoscopy & Vesicoscopy – Treating complex urinary tract issues.

His expertise extends to pediatric conditions such as birth defects, trauma, childhood cancers, and complex urological disorders. He is particularly renowned for managing challenging cases like Primary PSARP without colostomy for anorectal malformations.

Dr. Mohan has numerous publications in international journals, contributed to leading pediatric surgery textbooks, and was honored with the “Best Pediatric Surgical Service Provider of Andhra Pradesh” award by the NTR Healthcare Trust.

Dr. T. VITTAL MOHAN

Dr. T. VITTAL MOHAN

A Lifesaver for Children in Rayalaseema For over 20 years, Dr. T. Vittal Mohan, M.S., M.Ch., has been a beacon of hope for children in the Rayalaseema region of Andhra Pradesh. With a remarkable record of over 40,000 successful surgeries, he has transformed the lives of countless families. Specializing in minimally invasive procedures, he is a pioneer in laparoscopy, addressing appendicitis, hernias, and other abdominal issues with minimal scarring. His expertise in bronchoscopy has enabled life-saving interventions for airway obstructions, including foreign body aspiration. He is also highly skilled in thoracoscopy for chest surgeries, as well as cystoscopy and vesicoscopy for treating complex urinary tract issues. Dr. Mohan’s expertise extends to a wide range of pediatric conditions, including birth defects, trauma, childhood cancers, and complex urological disorders. He is particularly renowned for handling challenging cases such as Primary PSARP without colostomy for anorectal malformations. His commitment to excellence is evident in his numerous publications in international medical journals and his contributions to leading pediatric surgery textbooks. His dedication has been recognized with the prestigious "Best Pediatric Surgical Service Provider of Andhra Pradesh" award from the Andhra Pradesh State NTR Healthcare Trust. "His expertise and compassion are truly exceptional." If you are seeking expert and compassionate care for your child, Dr. T. Vittal Mohan is the ideal choice. Dr. NAVEEN KUMAR

Dr. NAVEEN KUMAR

Dr. Naveen Kumar Cheri, M.D., is a highly experienced Consultant Pediatrician with 12 years of expertise in pediatric and neonatal care. Currently practicing at Om Supatham Multi-speciality Hospital in Tirupati, he specializes in Pediatric Infectious Diseases as well as the pre-operative and post-operative management of pediatric and neonatal surgical conditions. Dr. Naveen has a keen interest in immunization and preventive child health, ensuring that children receive the best possible care for a healthy future. He earned his Doctor of Medicine (M.D.) in Pediatrics and M.B.B.S. from the prestigious S.V. Medical College, Tirupati, under Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences. Dedicated to compassionate and comprehensive care, Dr. Naveen is committed to improving the health and well-being of all children and newborns.
